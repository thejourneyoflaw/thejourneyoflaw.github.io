Notwithstanding精读

今日术语 Notwithstanding

1. 真实句子精读

英文原文： Notwithstanding any provision to the contrary in this Agreement, the Parties agree that the limitation of liability shall not apply to any breach of confidentiality obligations.

中文翻译： 尽管本协议有任何相反规定，双方同意，责任限制不适用于任何违反保密义务的行为。

🔍 语法拆解： (Notwithstanding any provision to the contrary in this Agreement), [the Parties] [agree] (that [the limitation of liability] [shall not apply] (to any breach of confidentiality obligations)).

  • 主干：[the Parties] [agree] （主谓结构）
